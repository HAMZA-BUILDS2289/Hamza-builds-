Client words

Questions

What do I need to send you?

Your logo, your brand colours, your list of services, a few photos and your phone and WhatsApp numbers. If you don't have a logo or photos yet, tell me and we will work around it.

Are hosting and a domain included?

Yes. In every package I host the site on Vercel, buy your domain and connect it, so the site is online under your own name.

How long will it take?

It depends on how many pages and features you want. I give you a date before I start, once I know what you need.

Can I change things after launch?

Yes. New sections, changes and fixes after launch are quoted separately for each job, so you only pay for what you ask for.

How do I pay?

We agree the price and how you pay before I start work. My prices are listed above in naira, with approximate dollar amounts.

Do you only work with clinics?

No. Clinics are most of my work, but I have also built sites for restaurants, a burger place and a car wash.
