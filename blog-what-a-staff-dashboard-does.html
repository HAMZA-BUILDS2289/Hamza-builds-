What a staff dashboard does for a busy clinic

How logins, rosters and QR check-in save time at the front desk.

A staff dashboard is a private part of your website that only your team can open. It turns a booking website into a system the whole clinic runs on.

Different people see different things

In the system I built for Na'ibi Dental Clinic there are four roles: admin, receptionist, dentist and technician. The receptionist sees every booking and can add one by hand. The dentist sees their own schedule. The admin can change services, prices and staff accounts.

Rosters decide who gets the booking

Each dentist and technician has a roster of working days and hours. When a patient books, the system assigns someone who is working at that time, so nobody is booked while on leave.

QR check-in

Every booking gets a QR code. When the patient arrives, the receptionist scans it or types the booking code, and the visit is marked as arrived.

Follow each treatment

A booking moves through clear stages: pending, confirmed, in treatment, completed, cancelled or no show. At the end of the day you can see what happened to every appointment.
