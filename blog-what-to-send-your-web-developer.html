What to send your web developer before you start

A short list that saves days of back and forth.

The fastest way to get your website is to send everything in one message. Here is the list I ask my clients for.

Your brand

Your logo as an image file, and your colours. If you do not know your colour codes, tell me which colours are on your logo and I will match them.

Your services

A list of every treatment or product you offer, with a line about each. Add prices if you want them on the site.

Photos

Your clinic, your team, your equipment. Photos taken on a phone in good light are fine. They make the site feel like yours.

Contact details

Your address, phone and WhatsApp numbers, email and opening hours. If you are on Google Maps, send the link.

Reviews

Your Google rating and a few comments from patients who agree to be quoted.

Your domain idea

The web address you would like, such as yourclinicname.com. I check if it is free and buy it for you.
