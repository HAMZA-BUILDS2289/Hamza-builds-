Questions

Can I see the website before I pay?

Yes. I send a preview link, you open it on your phone and tell me what to change. Hosting and a domain are only needed when you are ready to go live.

What do I need to send you?

Your business name, address, phone number, list of services or menu, photos if you have them, and the colours you like. I can use your logo if you have one.

Can I change the website myself later?

For the complete clinic system, yes. The admin adds, edits and prices services from a dashboard and the website updates. For other sites, send me the change on WhatsApp.

Do you only build for clinics?

No. Clinics are most of my work, but I have also built sites for restaurants, a takeaway and a car wash.
