Why every clinic needs a website

Patients search on their phones before they call. Here is what a clinic loses without a website.

When someone has a toothache or wants braces, the first thing they do is search on their phone. They look for a clinic near them, open two or three results and decide in a minute or two. If your clinic has no website, you are not one of those results.

Patients check before they call

A website answers the questions people would otherwise ask on the phone: where you are, what you treat, when you open and how to book. Every question a patient can answer on their own is a call your reception does not have to take.

It shows you are real

Photos of your clinic, your services and your reviews make a stranger comfortable walking in. A page with your Google rating and a map pin does more for trust than any flyer.

It works at night

Most people look for a dentist after work or late in the evening. A site with a WhatsApp or booking button collects those patients while your clinic is closed.

What to put on it

Start simple: your services, your address and opening hours, a few photos, your reviews, and one clear button to book. You can add a blog, a gallery and more later.
