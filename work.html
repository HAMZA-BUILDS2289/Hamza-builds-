Case study: Na'ibi Dental Clinic

A clinic in Kano that wanted patients to book online and staff to run the day from one system.

The need

Booking by phone and paper made it hard to avoid double bookings and to see who was free. The clinic wanted online booking and a way for staff to manage appointments.

What I built

A booking page for patients with no sign-in, automatic dentist and technician assignment from rosters, dashboards for each staff role, QR check-in, treatment progress and patient reviews.

What the clinic gets

Patients pick a time and get a booking reference and QR code. Staff see the day, confirm visits and mark treatments. The admin edits services and prices and the website updates.
