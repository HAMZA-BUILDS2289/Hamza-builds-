Online booking or WhatsApp booking: which is right for your clinic?

Two ways to let patients book, what each one does well, and how to choose.

Both options let patients book without calling. They suit different clinics, and many clinics start with one and add the other later.

WhatsApp booking

The patient taps a button and WhatsApp opens with a message already written. Your receptionist replies and confirms the time. It is quick to set up and patients already know how to use it. The weakness is that someone has to read and answer every message, and double bookings are easy to make by hand.

Online booking

The patient picks a treatment, a date and a time on your website. The system checks what is free, so two patients cannot take the same slot. It works at night and needs less of your staff's time. It costs more to build.

How to choose

If you see a handful of patients a day and your reception is always on WhatsApp, a WhatsApp button is enough. If you have several dentists, rooms or technicians, or you lose time to double bookings, online booking pays for itself.

You can have both

Patients who prefer WhatsApp can still use it, while those who want to book at midnight use the booking page. The Na'ibi Dental Clinic site offers both.
