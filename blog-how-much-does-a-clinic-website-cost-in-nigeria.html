How much does a clinic website cost in Nigeria?

What you pay for at each level, and what makes the price go up.

Prices vary a lot, and a cheap quote often leaves things out. These are my own rates, so you can see what each level includes.

A clinic website: ₦150,000 to ₦250,000

About $115 to $190. Your services, gallery, reviews, contact details and a WhatsApp button, designed for phones, with hosting and your domain included.

A website with booking: ₦300,000 to ₦450,000

About $225 to $340. Everything above, plus patients booking through WhatsApp or a booking page.

A full clinic system: ₦800,000 to ₦2,000,000

About $600 to $1,500. Staff logins for different roles, rosters, automatic assignment of dentists, QR check-in and treatment tracking.

What changes the price

The number of pages, how many staff roles you need, and whether you want features such as online payment or reminders. I quote a fixed price before I start.

Dollar amounts are approximate, at about ₦1,330 to $1.
