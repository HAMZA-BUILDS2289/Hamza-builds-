About me

A web developer who builds sites that work for the people running the business.

Hi, I'm Hamza.

I'm a web developer in Nigeria. I build websites for clinics, restaurants and local businesses, from simple business sites to complete booking systems.

Most of my work is for dental clinics in Kano, Abuja, Kaduna and Osogbo. I have also built sites for restaurants in Lagos and Ibadan and a car wash in the UK.

I like building things people use every day. For the Na'ibi Dental Clinic I built a booking system where patients book online and staff manage the day from their own dashboards.
