About me

Web developer in Ilorin, building for clinics and local businesses.

I'm Hamza, a web developer based in Ilorin, Kwara State. I build websites, online booking and staff systems for dental clinics and local businesses across Nigeria.

My clients are in Kano, Abuja, Lagos, Port Harcourt, Osogbo, Kaduna and Ibadan. Most are clinics, and I have also built sites for restaurants, a burger place and a car wash.

What I build

  • Websites with your services, gallery, reviews and a WhatsApp button, designed phone first.
  • Booking systems so patients and customers book online without calling.
  • Staff dashboards with logins, rosters, QR check-in and treatment tracking, like the one I built for Na'ibi Dental Clinic.

How I work

You deal with me directly, from the first message to launch. I design the site, build it, host it on Vercel, buy your domain and connect it. After launch I make changes when you need them, and each change is quoted separately.
