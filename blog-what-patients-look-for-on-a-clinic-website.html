Six things patients look for on a clinic website

Check your own site against this list.

When a patient opens a clinic website, they are looking for a few specific things. If they cannot find them in a few seconds, they leave.

1. Where you are

Your address and a map, near the top. Mention a landmark if your street is hard to find.

2. When you open

Opening hours on the home page, with weekends and public holidays clear.

3. What you treat

A simple list of services. Patients search for words like whitening, braces and implants, so use those words.

4. Proof that others trust you

Your Google rating and real reviews with names.

5. How to book

One obvious button that takes them to WhatsApp or the booking page, on every page.

6. A fast, clean phone layout

Text they can read without zooming and buttons they can tap with a thumb. Most of your visitors are on phones.
